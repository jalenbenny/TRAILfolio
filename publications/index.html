  1. A performance-based voting framework for assertion detection in clinical notes
    Behnaz Eslami, Dmitriy Dligach, Benjamin Strickland, and 2 more authors
    Data: MIMIC-III. Status: Completed. Contact: beslami@luc.edu
    Abs

    This paper presents a performance-based voting framework that combines multiple pre-trained NLP models (including BioBERT and BiLSTM-CNN-Char) to accurately detect and classify clinical assertions – polarity, and related categories – in clinical notes.

  2. Toward Digital Twins in the Intensive Care Unit: A Medication Management Case Study
    Behnaz Eslami, Majid Afshar, Samie Tootooni, and 4 more authors
    Data: MIMIC-III. Status: Completed. Contact: beslami@luc.edu
    Abs

    This study shows that fine-tuning LLaMA-3 with LoRA on specialty-specific ICU physician notes (medical ICU) produces more accurate digital twin treatment recommendations, outperforming other-specialty and general fine-tuning baselines.

  3. A Hybrid Language Framework for Ontology-Based Clinical Concept Extraction
    Behnaz Eslami, Dmitriy Dligach, Nazanin Azarvash, and 3 more authors
    Data: MIMIC-III. Status: Active (in progress). Contact: beslami@luc.edu
    Abs

    This study introduces a hybrid ontology-based framework combining SparkNLP, SentenceBERT embeddings, zero-shot LLMs (LLaMA3-8B and Mistral-7B), and UMLS/SNOMED CT normalization for clinical concept extraction.

  4. Mapping Free-Text Chief Complaints to a Structured Clinical Taxonomy Using LLM-Based Soft Classification
    Behnaz Eslami, Dmitriy Dligach, Nazanin Azarvash, and 3 more authors
    Data: Indiana University chief complaints dataset. Status: Active (in progress). Contact: beslami@luc.edu
    Abs

    This study introduces CCMapper-LLM, a zero-shot LLaMA-3.1-70B-based framework for mapping free-text emergency department chief complaints to a structured multi-label clinical taxonomy.

  5. Discovering Latent Clinical Patterns Through LLM-Based Topic Modeling and Large-Scale Concept Clustering
    Behnaz Eslami, Dmitriy Dligach, and Samie Tootooni
    Data: Loyola therapeutic inertia data. Status: Active (in progress). Contact: beslami@luc.edu
    Abs

    This study presents an LLM-based framework that combines topic modeling with large-scale concept clustering to uncover latent patterns in clinical text, offering a scalable approach for identifying hidden themes.

  6. Validating Caroli Disease/Syndrome Using LLM
    A. Kochari, S. Tootooni, and M. Saban
    Data: CaroliO ontology (OWL) and published PubMed literature; no patient-level data. Status: Active (in progress). Contact: akochari@luc.edu
    Abs

    This project uses an LLM to evaluate the medical content of the Caroli Disease and Syndrome Ontology (CaroliO), checking explicit ontology axioms and class relationships against biomedical evidence from PubMed.
