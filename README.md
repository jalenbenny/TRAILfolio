# TRAIL Lab

The TRAIL Lab works at the intersection of artificial intelligence, clinical data, and patient care, spanning stroke triage, drug dosing, clinical NLP, and AI evaluation in healthcare settings. The lab is led by Dr. Samie Tootooni, Assistant Professor in the Department of Health Informatics and Data Science at Loyola University Chicago.

The lab is closely tied to the Center for Health Outcomes and Informatics Research (CHOIR) at Loyola's Parkinson School of Health Sciences and Public Health, where Dr. Tootooni serves as Associate Center Director.

Our team brings together graduate research assistants, medical students, and undergraduates working across AI, NLP, and clinical informatics.
