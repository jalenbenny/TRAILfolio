Impact of Area Deprivation on Blood Pressure Control in Hypertensive Patients

Association between neighborhood area deprivation, blood pressure control, and therapeutic inertia in older hypertensive adults.

Health Systems, Equity & Access Completed
Team
M. Saban, S. Tootooni, T. Markossian, A. Wozniak, G. Hiura, B. Probst, K. Habicht, H. Kramer
Contact
msaban@luc.edu
Project READMEFrom the lab's project repository

Overview

This project investigates how neighborhood characteristics, measured through Area Deprivation Index (ADI), influences blood pressure control and therapeutic inertia in older adults with hypertension. This study analyzes data from primary care visits to understand the relationship between neighborhood disadvantage and hypertension management.

Objectives

Examine the association between Area Deprivation Index (ADI) and uncontrolled blood pressure during primary care clinic visits Investigate the relationship between ADI and therapeutic inertia during visits with uncontrolled blood pressure Evaluate how neighborhood characteristics influence hypertension management in older adults
