Prehospital Stroke Decision Support

These projects build and evaluate AI tools that help paramedics recognize stroke and choose the right hospital before the patient arrives. The work covers the prediction models, the narrative text those models can learn from, interviews with paramedics about how a tool would fit their workflow, and cost analyses of what correct and incorrect routing means for patients and hospitals.

AI in Medication Dosing in Critical Care

Dosing in the ICU has to account for changing kidney function, drug levels that are hard to measure directly, and a large amount of data in the record. These projects develop Bayesian pharmacokinetic engines, machine learning models trained on the labels those engines produce, and language model tools that explain model output to clinicians in plain words.

Bayesian two-compartment pharmacokinetic engine (Vanco24) for individualized vancomycin AUC24 estimation from EHR data.

Connects to: ML prediction of vancomycin AUC24, AI in critical care dosing (review)

Machine Learning Prediction of Vancomycin AUC24 Using Vanco24-Derived Labels in MIMIC-IV
