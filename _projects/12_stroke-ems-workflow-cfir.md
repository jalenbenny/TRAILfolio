CFIR-guided pre-implementation assessment of EMS workflow determinants for an AI stroke triage tool. 25 semi-structured interviews with 20 EMS participants across Illinois Chicagoland EMS regions, coded against the Consolidated Framework for Implementation Research to identify barriers and facilitators ahead of deployment.

**Data availability:** Transcripts are not shared. Repo holds the interview guide, codebook, and analysis documentation only.
