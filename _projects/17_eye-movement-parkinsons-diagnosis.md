Designed a computer vision pipeline to analyze eye-tracking video and extract clinical biomarkers such as saccade velocity and regain rate for Parkinson's diagnosis, enabling quantitative, non-invasive assessment of ocular motor behavior to support early detection.

Aimed at a scalable, non-invasive diagnostic aid through automated video analysis rather than in-clinic specialized equipment.
