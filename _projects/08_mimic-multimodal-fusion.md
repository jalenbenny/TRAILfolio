Benchmarks unimodal baselines against three fusion strategies on MIMIC-IV, combining tabular models over structured records with transformer encoders over discharge notes.

**Data availability:** MIMIC-IV requires PhysioNet credentials and a data use agreement. No data in repo, just code/configs.
