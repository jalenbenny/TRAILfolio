Association between neighborhood area deprivation, blood pressure control, and therapeutic inertia in older hypertensive adults. A retrospective EHR analysis linking patient addresses to the Area Deprivation Index to test whether neighborhood disadvantage predicts uncontrolled hypertension and delayed treatment intensification.

**Data availability:** EHR data is restricted under IRB and contains PHI, so it is not shared. ADI values are publicly available through the Neighborhood Atlas.
