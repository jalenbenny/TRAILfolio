## Synopsis
Uses an LLM to evaluate the medical content of the Caroli Disease and Syndrome Ontology (CaroliO), examining explicit ontology axioms and class relationships against PubMed literature.

## Data Availability
Uses the basic CaroliO OWL ontology and published PubMed literature. No patient-level data or protected health information.
