Early, intermediate, and late fusion of structured EHR data with clinical notes for 30-day mortality prediction. Benchmarks unimodal baselines against three fusion strategies on MIMIC-IV, combining tabular models over structured records with transformer encoders over discharge summaries.

**Data availability:** MIMIC-IV requires PhysioNet credentials and a data use agreement. No data in the repo, code and configs only.
