## Synopsis
Models the downstream cost and outcome consequences of correct vs. incorrect prehospital stroke routing under an AI decision support tool. Parameters are drawn from the literature.

## Data Availability
Model inputs are derived from published literature and licensed datasets. Tables, code, etc. should be in the repo.
