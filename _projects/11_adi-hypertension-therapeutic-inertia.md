Retrospective EHR analysis linking patient addresses to the Area Deprivation Index to test whether neighborhood disadvantage predicts uncontrolled hypertension and clinician therapeutic inertia.

**Data availability:** EHR data is restricted under IRB and has PHI, so it's not shared. ADI values are publicly available through the Neighborhood Atlas.
