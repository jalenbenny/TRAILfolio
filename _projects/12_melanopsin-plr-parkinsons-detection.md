Applies computer vision to infrared pupillometry video to isolate the melanopsin-dependent post-illumination pupillary light reflex (PIPR), a retinal biomarker linked to Parkinson's disease.

**Data availability:** Pupillometry video and derived reflex measurements are not shared due to participant privacy. Repo holds analysis code only.
