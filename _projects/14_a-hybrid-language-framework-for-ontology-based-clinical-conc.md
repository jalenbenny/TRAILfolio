## Synopsis
Combines SparkNLP, SentenceBERT embeddings, zero-shot LLMs (LLaMA3-8B and Mistral-7B), and UMLS/SNOMED CT normalization for clinical concept extraction.

## Data Availability
MIMIC-III dataset.
