Decision model of the cost impact of implementing AI-based prehospital stroke triage. Models the downstream cost and outcome consequences of correct versus incorrect prehospital stroke routing under an AI decision support tool, with parameters drawn from published literature and institutional data.

**Data availability:** Model inputs are derived from published literature and licensed datasets. Tables and code belong in the repo, but not the underlying licensed data itself.
