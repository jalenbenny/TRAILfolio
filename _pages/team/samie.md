Samie Tootooni is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Health Informatics and Data Science, with a secondary appointment in the Center for Health Outcomes and Informatics Research at Loyola University Chicago. He received his PhD in Industrial and Systems Engineering (ISE) from Binghamton University in 2016, and prior to joining Loyola was a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Health Sciences Research at Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota, for two years. His research lies at the intersection of decision science and artificial intelligence, advancing monitoring and predictive analytics for complex healthcare systems, with particular interest in how data science and AI tools such as machine learning and natural language processing support clinical decision support models. Drawing on his Industrial and Systems Engineering background, he also applies systems thinking, quality assurance, and optimization principles to improve health outcomes, and teaches courses on ontologies (knowledge representation) in health and natural language processing.
