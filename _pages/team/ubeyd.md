Ubeyd Dincer is a Research Assistant in the Parkinson School of Health Sciences and Public Health, and recently completed his M.S. in Business Data Analytics at Loyola University Chicago. His primary research focuses on healthcare analytics, health economics, and AI-driven decision support systems. He currently leads a stroke triage project investigating the clinical and economic burden of patient mis-triage using nationwide emergency department data (HCUP). His broader interests include healthcare operations, predictive modeling, and the use of data science to improve patient outcomes.
